About

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of Gothenburg, supervised by Prof. Romaric Duvignau and Prof. Marina Papatriantafilou. My research focuses on communication-efficient algorithms for continuously monitoring large-scale distributed systems, as part of the READY project (Rethinking Monitoring for Large Distributed Systems).
